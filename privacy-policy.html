Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

🔒 Short version: DealPulse does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All processing happens locally in your browser.

1. What We Collect

DealPulse does not collect any personally identifiable information. The extension reads product data only from the current tab when you are on a supported e-commerce site:

  • Product title
  • Current price displayed on the page
  • Product ID (ASIN for Amazon, PID for Flipkart)
  • Current page URL (to detect product pages)

2. How We Use Data

All data read from the page is used only to display price comparison information within the extension widget. It is:

  • Processed locally in your browser
  • Never sent to any external server
  • Never stored beyond your current browsing session
  • Never shared with third parties

3. Permissions Explained

DealPulse requests the following browser permissions:

  • activeTab — To read product information from the currently open tab
  • storage — To save your price alert preferences locally on your device
  • Host permissions (amazon.in, flipkart.com) — To inject the price widget only on supported shopping sites

4. Cookies & Tracking

DealPulse does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or any form of behavioral tracking. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics service.

5. Data Storage

If you set a price alert, that preference is stored using Chrome's local storage API on your own device. This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared at any time by removing the extension.

6. Third-Party Services

DealPulse does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or data brokers. All price comparison data shown is for informational purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

DealPulse is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
